Who we are

Children and Youths Advocate Foundation in Africa (CYAFA South Sudan) was founded to improve and uphold the rights of children and youth in communities across Africa. We work with high need, low resource families to prevent child abuse, reduce child poverty, and give every child a better chance in life.

LiveOngoing project

Scholarship And Education Support For Primary And Secondary Learners In Magwi County

We are helping vulnerable learners in Magwi County stay in school, including orphans and children from families facing financial hardship.

Our work

Practical programs that help children learn, families stay healthy, and young people and women earn a living.

All programs

Education

New community schools and upgrades to existing ones, with a special focus on the girl child.
  • Health & WASHMaternal and child health, immunization, safe drinking water and sanitation.
  • Women's EmpowermentSelf help groups, income generating activities and vocational training.
  • LivelihoodsAccess to credit and group savings for small enterprises.

We also run: Scholarship Programme, Youth Empowerment, Child Protection & Legal Aid, Environment & Agriculture, Peacebuilding & Human Rights, Emergency Response, Drug Abuse Prevention and the Resource Center.
