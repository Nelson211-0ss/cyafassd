CYAFA Scholarship Programme

Expanding Access to Education. Empowering Vulnerable Children. Building Brighter Futures.

Apply for Scholarship Support a Student

Education should not depend on what a family can afford

CYAFA works to improve access to education for children and young people facing financial hardship, orphanhood, disability and other vulnerabilities.

Poverty, the loss of parents, disability, emergencies and gender-related barriers keep many learners out of the classroom, or force them to leave before they finish. We believe financial difficulties should not prevent deserving students from continuing their education.

The Scholarship Programme helps vulnerable and deserving learners access, continue and progress through school, higher education, or recognized vocational training.

Implementation focus: Magwi County, South Sudan

The loss of parents and the burden of poverty should not end a child’s educational journey.
Our flagship scholarship

Orphans and Extremely Vulnerable Children Scholarship

Supports learners who have lost both parents and face serious financial hardship, or who lack adequate support to continue their education.

Support may go toward school or tuition fees, uniforms, books, learning materials, examination fees, and other approved essential educational costs.

Who can apply

Scholarship categories

Alongside our flagship scholarship, CYAFA supports learners through seven further categories. Each has its own eligibility criteria, and sponsors may choose the category they wish to support.

02

Financially Disadvantaged Students

Helps eligible students from low-income households who struggle to meet essential education costs.

03

Girls’ Education

Promotes girls’ access to, retention in, and completion of education.

04

Academic Excellence and Talent

Recognizes eligible students demonstrating strong academic performance or exceptional talent, subject to programme criteria and available funding.

05

University and Tertiary Education

Assists eligible learners pursuing university, college, or other recognized post-secondary education.

06

Technical and Vocational Skills

Supports eligible young people undertaking practical, technical, or vocational training.

07

Inclusive Education

Helps address education barriers faced by children and young people with disabilities or additional learning needs.

08

Emergency Education Support

Provides appropriate education assistance to eligible students facing sudden crises that threaten their continued schooling.

Who can apply?

Applications may be made by the learner or, where appropriate, by a parent, guardian, caregiver, school representative, or authorized community representative.

Eligibility does not automatically guarantee an award. Selection depends on the assessment process and available resources.

  • Hardship or vulnerability

    Learners who face demonstrable financial hardship or significant vulnerability.

  • Education at risk

    Orphans, or learners in other circumstances that place their education at risk, where this applies to the scholarship category.

  • Recognized institution

    Enrolled, admitted, or seeking to continue education at a recognized institution, according to the category requirements.

  • Supporting information

    Able to provide the information and documents reasonably required for assessment.

  • Category criteria

    Meeting the published eligibility criteria for the relevant scholarship category.

How the programme works

  1. Review the categories

    Read the scholarship categories and eligibility requirements.

  2. Submit an application

    Apply through the officially approved application channel.

  3. Review

    CYAFA reviews the application and any supporting information.

  4. Assessment

    Eligible applications are assessed using transparent criteria.

  5. Notification

    Selected applicants are contacted through the official contact details provided.

An ongoing commitment to education

The CYAFA Scholarship Programme is intended to operate on a continuing basis, rather than being limited to a predetermined six-month or one-year period. Individual support is reviewed according to the applicable scholarship criteria, the student’s circumstances, programme requirements, and available funding.

Help a Student Stay in School

We invite individuals, community members, diaspora communities, businesses, and institutional partners to contribute to education support.

Depending on the programme’s approved support arrangements, contributions may help eligible students meet education-related costs such as:

  • School fees
  • Learning materials
  • Uniforms
  • Examination fees
  • Other necessary expenses

Sponsors may choose the scholarship category they wish to support. Donor reports protect children’s identities.

Safeguarding, fairness and privacy

CYAFA aims to assess applications fairly, protect applicants’ personal information, and prioritize children’s safety and dignity.

Published criteria applied consistently, with conflicts of interest declared

Sensitive information shared only with authorized people who need it

No applicant names, records or family details published without proper consent

Need to raise a concern?

You can report misconduct, discrimination or misuse of funds in confidence. Every concern is handled promptly, impartially and confidentially.
