Who we are

Giving every child a better chance in life

Children and Youths Advocate Foundation in Africa (CYAFA South Sudan) was founded to improve and uphold the rights of children and youth in communities across Africa.

We work with high need, low resource families to prevent child abuse, reduce child poverty, and give every child a better chance in life.

More about us

LiveOngoing project

Scholarship And Education Support For Primary And Secondary Learners In Magwi County

We are helping vulnerable learners in Magwi County stay in school, including orphans and children from families facing financial hardship.

What we do

Our work

Practical programs that help children learn, families stay healthy, and young people and women earn a living.

Education Education New community schools and upgrades to existing ones, with a special focus on the girl child. Health Health & WASH Maternal and child health, immunization, safe drinking water and sanitation. Women Women's Empowerment Self help groups, income generating activities and vocational training. Livelihoods Livelihoods Access to credit and group savings for small enterprises.

A message from our Founder & Executive Director

When a child is protected, educated and given a fair chance, a whole community grows stronger.

That belief is why CYAFA exists. Every day, our team works alongside families in South Sudan to keep children safe, keep learners in school, and help young people and women build a better future.

Thank you for standing with us. Together, we can give every child a better chance in life.

Nelson O. Ochan Our story
